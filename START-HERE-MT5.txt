Trading Journal AI, set up for MetaTrader 5 (Ava) gold trading
================================================================

1. Install once (if not already on this PC):
   - Python 3.11 or newer: https://www.python.org/downloads/  (tick "Add python.exe to PATH")
   - Node.js LTS: https://nodejs.org/

2. Double-click setup.bat (once). It installs everything and creates backend\.env.

3. Double-click launch.bat (every time). Then open http://localhost:3010 in your browser.

4. In the app: add an account (for example "Ava MT5", broker "MetaTrader 5"),
   then go to Import, choose "MetaTrader 5 (MT5)" and drop your MT5 report.

Exporting from MT5:
   History tab -> right-click -> Report -> save as Excel (.xlsx) or HTML.
   You can import the same report again at any time: trades already in the journal are skipped.

Times:
   The MT5 report is in UTC. backend\.env has MT5_TIME_OFFSET_HOURS=2, which moves
   every trade to South African time. Change it only if your broker's report time changes.

Gold only, in rand:
   Only XAUUSD / GOLD positions are imported; anything else in the report is ignored.
   Profit and loss come straight from MT5's Profit (+ Swap) column, in rand, exactly as Ava booked them.
   Gold prices, stops and targets stay in US dollars. Where the app works out money from prices
   (the risk of a stop-loss you enter, and "what if" exits), it converts dollars to rand with
   REACT_APP_USD_TO_ACCOUNT=16.2 in frontend\.env (the rate Ava used on your Aug-Sep trades).
   Change that number if the rand moves a lot, then restart launch.bat.

Optional:
   ANTHROPIC_API_KEY in backend\.env turns on the AI diary analysis, Day Review and Brain chat.

Gold charts on every trade:
   Each trade page shows a gold chart in SA time with your fills, entry/exit lines and EMA 9/21.
   Prices come from, in this order:
   1. Your TradingView price file (Import page -> "Gold price data for charts"). Claude exports
      FOREX.com XAUUSD candles from TradingView for you; drop the .csv there. Top it up every
      week or two, because TradingView only returns the most recent weeks of 5-minute candles.
   2. Dukascopy, downloaded automatically (free, no account) for anything the file does not cover.
      Downloads are saved, so each day is only fetched once. Needs an internet connection.
   The line under each chart says which source it used.

Your data stays on this PC in backend\trading_journal.db. Back it up by copying that one file.
